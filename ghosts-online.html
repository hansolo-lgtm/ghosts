How to play

Each player hides eight ghosts: four good, four evil. You can see which of yours is which. Your opponent cannot.

Win any one of three ways

  1. Escape. Walk a good ghost onto a door in a far corner, then step out on your next turn.
  2. Capture all four of your opponent's good ghosts.
  3. Lose all four of your own evil ghosts to capture.

Careful: if you capture all four of their evil ghosts, you lose.

On your turn

  • Move one ghost one square up, down, left or right.
  • Moving onto an enemy ghost captures it. Its true nature is revealed to both players.
  • You cannot move onto your own ghosts. Evil ghosts cannot escape, but they can bluff.

Good to know

  • Tap an enemy ghost, with none of yours selected, to mark a hunch: blue, red, or clear.
  • A ghost standing on a door can be captured before it leaves.
  • If 100 moves pass with no capture, the game is a draw.

Resign this game?

Your opponent wins and every ghost is revealed.
